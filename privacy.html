Tessa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Tessa is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Brent Reynolds. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What Tessa accesses

With the owner's consent, Tessa accesses the owner's own Google Drive files, Google Sheets spreadsheets and Gmail messages. It reads, searches, creates and edits these items only to do tasks the owner asks for, such as finding an email, updating a spreadsheet, or drafting a message.

How data is used and stored

Data from the owner's Google account is used only to carry out the owner's requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Access tokens are stored on the owner's own server and in the owner's own Google Drive and are not shared.

Google API Services

Tessa's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke Tessa's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and can delete stored tokens and data at will.
